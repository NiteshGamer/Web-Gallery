🎵 Playlist Builder

Turn a folder of songs into music/playlist.txt in one step.

  1. Put your audio files in the music folder (next to playlist.txt).
  2. Below, choose all of those same files at once (Ctrl/Cmd‑A in the picker selects everything).
  3. Download the generated file and use it to replace music/playlist.txt.

Tip: titles are auto‑generated from filenames (our_first_dance.mp3 → "Our First Dance"). To set a custom title for any song, edit its line in the box above so it reads filename.mp3 | Custom Title before downloading/copying.
